- Customize the right-click menu with your own commands, submenus, and Material Symbols. Open Right-click → Customize to edit `material-osc-menu.conf`.
- Adjust tone mapping, gamut mapping, and peak detection in Video Settings when playing HDR video.
- Adjust primary and secondary subtitle heights independently. Reset restores the subtitle settings from your mpv configuration. The subtitle button now reflects embedded closed captions.
- Keep the controls visible while the pointer rests on them with `keep_controls_on_hover`, open the volume slider sideways with `volume_slider=horizontal`, or follow the Windows accent color with `accent_color=system`.
- Remove entries from the playlist using the button that appears when you hover over a row. The media files stay on disk.
- Audio track labels show the channel layout. YouTube quality choices load sooner, use clearer labels for non-16:9 videos, and leave Settings usable while a quality switch is in progress.
- Improved mpv startup and local file loading. Title detection with GuessIt now runs in the background and can be disabled with `guessit=no`.
- Fixed missed rapid clicks and window movement while dragging sliders in mpv.net, clicks intercepted by edge seeking, and fullscreen behavior in borderless windows.
- Fixed the loading indicator flashing during quick seeks, quality detection when the video output is scaled, and the hover shape for a standalone playlist button.
- Added a Nix flake, Home Manager installation instructions, and GPLv3 licensing for material-osc.
